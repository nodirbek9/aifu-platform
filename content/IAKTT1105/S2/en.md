## Purpose of the seminar

Where does economic information come from and where does it go? Every purchase at a till is entered through a barcode scanner, paid through a terminal, printed on a receipt printer and stored on a server. Well-chosen devices reduce errors, speed up service and make reporting reliable. In the seminar we review the types of devices and choose a set of equipment for a small business.

:::note
**After this seminar you will be able to:**

- name the main types of input, output and storage devices;
- explain the use of each in economic tasks with examples;
- compare storage devices by capacity, speed, reliability and price;
- choose a set of equipment for a company on a sound basis.
:::

## Input devices

| Device | Role | Economic use |
|---|---|---|
| **Keyboard, mouse, touch screen** | manual input | documents, spreadsheets, the cash register interface |
| **Document scanner and OCR** | turning a paper document into an image, and into text through OCR | digitizing contracts and invoices |
| **Barcode and QR reader** | reading a product code quickly and without errors | tills, warehouses, stocktaking |
| **RFID reader** | reading radio tags at a distance | warehouses, logistics, access cards |
| **Payment terminal** (POS) | payment by bank card, NFC and QR | cashless trade |
| **Cameras and sensors** | image, temperature, weight, meters | quality control, scales, smart meters (IoT) |
| **Microphone** | voice commands and speech-to-text | call centres, voice assistants |

## Output devices

| Device | Features | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Monitor** | size, resolution (Full HD, 4K) | workplaces, two monitors for analysis |
| **Laser printer** | fast, low cost per page | printing many documents and reports |
| **Inkjet printer** | cheap colour printing, higher cost per page | small volumes of colour material |
| **Thermal printer** (receipt printer) | inkless, prints with heat | receipts, labels, barcodes |
| **Customer display** | shows the price to the buyer | tills |
| **Projector, interactive panel** | a large screen | presentations, meetings |

**Online cash registers and fiscal modules**: in Uzbekistan online cash registers (or virtual cash registers) are used in trade and services; the data of every receipt is sent through a fiscal module to the Tax Committee in real time, and the receipt carries a QR code for verification. This increases the transparency of tax revenue.

## Storage devices

| Device | Advantage | Drawback | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HDD** | large capacity, cheap (low price per GB) | slower, sensitive to shocks | archives, backups |
| **SSD** | very fast, durable, silent | higher price per GB | the OS, working programs and databases |
| **Flash drive, memory card** | portable, cheap | easy to lose or infect | moving files temporarily |
| **NAS** (network storage) | a shared disk for the office, several disks (RAID) | needs setup and protection | small office files and backups |
| **Cloud storage** | access from anywhere, the provider makes backups | needs the Internet and a subscription, a privacy question | collaboration, remote work, offsite backup |

An important principle: data is stored **in at least two places** (for example, a computer + a NAS or the cloud) — a single disk failure must not destroy all the records (backups, the 3-2-1 rule).

## Practical task: equipment for a shop

A small grocery shop is opening (1 till, 1 storeroom, the owner views reports remotely). The budget is limited.

1. List the devices needed for the till (an online cash register or a computer with cash register software, a barcode scanner, a receipt printer, a payment terminal, a customer display — which are mandatory and which optional?).
2. Choose a device for stock accounting (a mobile data collection terminal or a smartphone with a scanner) and justify your choice.
3. Propose a storage and backup scheme: where is the data stored, and where is the backup?
4. Find the approximate price of each device in local online shops and calculate the total cost.
5. What solution is needed for the owner to view reports remotely?

## Discussion questions

- Why is a barcode scanner not only faster than manual entry but also economically beneficial?
- Is cloud storage always cheaper than your own disk? In which cases is it not?
- What do online cash registers and fiscal modules give the state and the entrepreneur?

:::warning
**Common mistakes**

- **Choosing the cheapest device.** A scanner or printer that breaks often causes queues and losses.
- **Keeping data on one disk.** If the disk fails, all the records are lost.
- **Mixing up bits and bytes, resolution and size.** Read technical specifications carefully.
- **Using a flash drive as the main storage.** It is easily lost and infected.
:::

:::tip
Observe the checkout process in a supermarket: which input and output devices are used, and how long does one purchase take? Use your observation in the seminar task.
:::

## Summary

- Input: keyboards, scanners and OCR, barcode/QR, RFID, payment terminals, sensors.
- Output: monitors, laser, inkjet and thermal printers, displays.
- Storage: HDD (archives), SSD (speed), flash memory (transfer), NAS (office), cloud (remote access).
- Online cash registers and fiscal modules send sales data to the tax authority in real time.
- Data is stored in at least two places; equipment is chosen by price, reliability and purpose.
