## Internet banking

**Internet banking** (and mobile banking) means using bank services remotely through a website or an app.

| Service group | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Accounts and cards** | balance and statement, opening a virtual card, blocking a card, limits |
| **Payments and transfers** | card-to-card, utility and government payments, international money transfers, QR payments |
| **Loans and deposits** | online microloans, opening and topping up deposits, loan repayment schedules |
| **For business** | a corporate account, payment orders, payroll projects, currency exchange |

- **Remote identification**: the customer is verified without visiting the bank, through passport data and face biometrics (systems such as MyID).
- **Digital banks** — banks without branches that work only through an app.
- **Open Banking** — transferring bank data to other services (for example, an accounting program) through an API with the customer's consent.

**Benefits for the bank**: lower branch costs, 24/7 service, fast scoring. **For the customer**: saved time, no queues, lower fees.

## Internet trade

Internet trade channels: your own website and app, marketplaces, social networks and messengers (Telegram shops, Instagram), and omnichannel — online and offline shops together (order online, pick up in store).

**The process**: catalogue and search → basket → payment (card, payment systems, instalments, cash) → delivery or a pick-up point → returns and reviews. At every stage the customer may "drop out" — that is why simplifying the process matters.

## Internet insurance

- **Online policies**: compulsory motor third-party liability insurance (OSAGO), travel and property insurance — issued in a few minutes on a website or in an app; an electronic policy is equivalent to a paper one.
- **Online claims**: sending photos and documents through an app, tracking the payout status.
- **Telematics insurance**: a device in the car or a phone measures driving style; a careful driver gets a cheaper policy.
- **Insurtech** — new technologies in insurance: scoring, chatbots, parametric insurance (S12).

## Internet marketing

| Tool | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | raising a website in search engine results (content, speed, links) |
| **Contextual advertising** | ads matching a search query (Google Ads, Yandex Direct) |
| **Targeted advertising** | ads on social networks for an audience selected by age, location and interests |
| **SMM** | running pages on social networks, content, community |
| **Email and messenger mailings** | messages and promotions for existing customers, with their consent |
| **Content and influencer marketing** | useful articles and videos, cooperation with bloggers |

The **sales funnel** is the set of stages in the customer journey: saw (impression) → clicked (visit) → added to basket → bought → bought again. By calculating the share at each stage we find where many customers are lost. For example: 100,000 impressions → 2,000 visits (CTR 2%) → 300 baskets → 60 purchases → 15 repeat purchases. If the biggest loss is at the "basket → purchase" stage, the payment process should be checked.

## Rules for safe use

- Do not tell SMS codes, a card's CVV code or passwords to **anyone** — bank staff never ask for them.
- Install a bank app only from an official store (App Store, Google Play); do not trust "bank pages" arriving through links.
- Turn on two-step verification and card limits; use a separate card for the Internet.
- Messages such as "a prize", "compensation" or "your account is blocked" are often fraud. When in doubt, call the bank's official number yourself.
- Take out an insurance policy only on the official website or app of a licensed insurance company.

## Case: the "Shirin" confectionery company

The company has 3 shops in Tashkent and wants to start selling online. It has an Instagram page (12,000 followers), but orders are taken only by phone. Payment is in cash. There is no delivery. The company wants to open a business account with a bank, accept online payments and insure cars for delivery.

**Group tasks**:

1. Which internet trade channels should be chosen (own website, a Telegram bot, a marketplace)? Justify.
2. Which internet banking services are needed (a business account, accepting payments, payroll)?
3. Which types of insurance are needed for the delivery cars and the goods?
4. Draw up a one-month internet marketing plan: channels, budget allocation, metrics (conversion, CAC, ROAS — M13).
5. Draw the sales funnel and give ideas for improving the share at each stage.

:::warning
**Common mistakes**

- **Relying on a single channel.** If the platform's rules or prices change, sales stop.
- **Paying attention only to the top of the funnel.** Many visits — but few purchases because of an inconvenient payment process.
- **Mailing customers without their consent.** A breach of the law and lost subscribers.
- **Ignoring security.** Stealing money through fake pages and by asking for SMS codes is widespread.
:::

:::tip
Group all the services in the bank app you use into a table and mark which of them require remote identification.
:::

## Summary

- Internet banking: accounts, payments, loans and deposits, business services; remote identification and open banking.
- Internet trade: own website, marketplaces, social networks, omnichannel; a simple process affects conversion.
- Internet insurance: online policies, online claims, telematics.
- Internet marketing: SEO, contextual and targeted ads, SMM, mailings, content; effectiveness is measured with the funnel and metrics.
- Never giving SMS codes and card data to anyone is the main security rule.
