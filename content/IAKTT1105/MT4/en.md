## Purpose of this independent study

In M5 we saw a list of presentation programs. In this independent study we **compare them systematically**: which one suits which situation, what artificial intelligence has changed, and how an organization can choose a tool on a sound basis.

:::note
**After this independent study you will be able to:**

- classify presentation editors by type;
- compare the main editors against criteria;
- assess the capabilities and risks of artificial intelligence tools;
- make a justified choice with a weighted scoring matrix.
:::

## Types of editors

| Type | Examples | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Desktop** | Microsoft PowerPoint, Apple Keynote, LibreOffice Impress | full functionality, offline work |
| **Cloud** | Google Slides, PowerPoint for the web | in the browser, real-time co-editing, autosave |
| **Design platforms** | Canva, Pitch | ready templates, image and icon libraries, easy design |
| **Non-linear (zoom)** | Prezi | movement across one large "canvas", no slide sequence |
| **AI-based** | Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Tome | automatic slides from text, "smart" layouts |
| **Code-based** | reveal.js, Marp, LaTeX Beamer | a presentation from text or code (Markdown, LaTeX); convenient for formulas and versioning |

## Comparing the main editors

| Editor | Pricing model | Collaboration | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PowerPoint** | Microsoft 365 subscription; the web version is free (limited) | via OneDrive | the most features, the corporate standard, Copilot | the full version is paid |
| **Google Slides** | free (Google account) | the most convenient, real-time | in the browser, collaboration, Gemini | complex animation and design are limited |
| **Keynote** | free on Apple devices | via iCloud | beautiful templates and animations | convenient only in the Apple ecosystem |
| **LibreOffice Impress** | free, open source | limited | no licence, offline, the ODP format | fewer designs and templates |
| **Canva** | free + Pro subscription | real-time | templates, graphics library, Magic tools | complex data charts are limited |
| **Prezi** | free (limited) + subscription | yes | non-linear, the "zoom" effect | a lot of motion — dizzying, rare in business |
| **Gamma** | free (limited) + subscription | yes | a presentation from text in minutes | the result must always be checked |

## Artificial intelligence features

- **Creation**: drafting slides from text, a document or notes (PowerPoint Copilot, Gemini in Slides, Gamma).
- **Design**: automatic layout and colour suggestions (PowerPoint Designer, Canva Magic Design).
- **Text**: shortening, changing the tone, translation, writing speaker notes.
- **Images**: generating and editing pictures, removing backgrounds.
- **Rehearsal**: a coach analysing speaking pace, filler words and reading slide text aloud (PowerPoint Speaker Coach).

:::warning
**Risks of artificial intelligence**: invented figures and facts ("hallucinations"), outdated information, the same "template" look, uploading confidential data to an external service. Check every figure against its source and upload confidential reports only to tools your organization has approved. You are responsible for the presentation.
:::

## Formats and compatibility

| Format | Program | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **PPTX** | PowerPoint (almost everything reads it) | the de facto standard; fonts and animations may change in other programs |
| **ODP** | LibreOffice Impress | an open standard |
| **KEY** | Keynote | exported as PPTX or PDF for other platforms |
| **PDF** | all | the most reliable for distribution: looks the same everywhere, but animations are lost |

:::tip
Before presenting on someone else's computer: embed the fonts in the file or use standard fonts, include videos in the file, and put a backup PDF copy on a USB stick.
:::

## The weighted scoring matrix

When choosing a tool for an organization, use criteria and their weights, not gut feeling. Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5 and multiplied by its weight.

**Example**: a consulting company of 30 people whose staff often work remotely.

| Criterion (weight) | PowerPoint | Google Slides | Canva | Impress | Keynote |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price (25%) | 3 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| Collaboration (25%) | 4 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Features (20%) | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| Offline work (10%) | 5 | 2 | 2 | 5 | 5 |
| Artificial intelligence (10%) | 4 | 3 | 5 | 1 | 2 |
| Ease of learning (10%) | 4 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 4 |
| **Weighted score** | **4.05** | **4.10** | **3.80** | **3.25** | **3.65** |

The calculation, for example for Google Slides: 5·0.25 + 5·0.25 + 3·0.20 + 2·0.10 + 3·0.10 + 5·0.10 = 4.10. Result: Google Slides and PowerPoint are almost equal. If clients require PPTX or the company already uses Microsoft 365, choose PowerPoint; otherwise Google Slides. If the weights change, the result changes too — so agree on the weights with management in advance.

:::warning
**Common mistakes**

- **Choosing because "everyone uses it".** Work out the organization's needs and the TCO (M2).
- **Not checking format compatibility.** A file sent to a client may open "broken" in their program.
- **Using unlicensed software.** A legal and security risk (M3).
- **Presenting artificial intelligence output without checking it.**
:::

## Assignment

1. Prepare a 5-slide presentation on one topic (for example, "E-commerce in Uzbekistan", M13) in two different editors: one traditional (PowerPoint, Google Slides or Impress) and one design or AI platform (Canva or Gamma).
2. Write down how long each took, the quality of the result and what corrections were needed.
3. Build a weighted scoring matrix for an organization of your choice (at least 4 editors, 5 criteria) and give a recommendation.

## Summary

- Types of editors: desktop, cloud, design platforms, non-linear, AI-based, code-based.
- PowerPoint — features and the corporate standard; Google Slides — collaboration; Canva — design; Impress — free and open; Keynote — the Apple ecosystem.
- Artificial intelligence speeds up slides, design and text, but the result must be checked and confidentiality respected.
- The choice is based on a weighted scoring matrix and the TCO.
