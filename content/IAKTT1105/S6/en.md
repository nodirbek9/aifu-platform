## Purpose of the seminar

In lecture M6 we looked at networks and Internet services in general. In the seminar we analyze how they create value in **specific sectors of the national economy** and propose a network solution for one sector.

:::note
**After this seminar you will be able to:**

- explain the use of network technologies in the main economic sectors with examples;
- know the role of networks in payment and tax systems;
- assess the economic effect of IoT and remote services;
- propose a network solution for a sector.
:::

## Network technologies in economic sectors

| Sector | Network solutions | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Banking and payments** | bank card networks (Uzcard, Humo, Visa, Mastercard), international transfers (SWIFT), the Central Bank's payment system, mobile payments and QR | payment in seconds, less cash in circulation |
| **Tax and customs** | online cash registers and fiscal modules, electronic invoices, electronic customs declarations | real-time control, transparency, saving entrepreneurs' time |
| **Trade** | online shops, marketplaces, a central database between warehouses and tills | new markets, precise stock management |
| **Logistics and transport** | GPS tracking, electronic waybills, online rail and air tickets | fuel and time savings, transparent delivery |
| **Industry** | sensors and IoT, SCADA systems (remote process control), ERP | continuous monitoring, preventing accidents |
| **Agriculture** | soil and moisture sensors, smart irrigation, satellite and drone data | saving water and fertilizer, higher yields |
| **Energy and utilities** | smart electricity, gas and water meters, remote meter reading | fewer losses, accurate billing |
| **Healthcare** | electronic medical records, telemedicine, online queues | remote access to doctors, time savings |
| **Education** | distance learning platforms, electronic libraries | education reaching the regions |

## Digital infrastructure in Uzbekistan

- The expansion of optical fibre networks and mobile Internet coverage, 4G and 5G networks.
- The public services portal (my.gov.uz), the electronic account of the tax service, the electronic invoice system.
- Domestic payment systems (Uzcard, Humo), mobile payment apps (Click, Payme, Uzum Bank and others), the Central Bank's instant payment system.
- IT Park, data centres and projects within the "Digital Uzbekistan — 2030" strategy (M1).

Note: indicators change quickly — in essays and presentations use the latest data of the Ministry of Digital Technologies, the Central Bank and the Statistics Agency.

## Case: a regional agricultural cooperative

The cooperative has 500 hectares of cotton and grain. Problems: high water use, the irrigation schedule is drawn up by hand, it is hard to know where the machinery is, harvest and cost records are kept in a notebook, the Internet is available only in the office.

**Group tasks**:

1. Propose a network solution for the cooperative: which sensors, which communication channel (mobile, a low-power wireless network such as LoRaWAN, satellite), where is the data stored?
2. Which indicators improve (water, fuel, harvest) and how are they measured?
3. Write down the approximate costs and expected savings, estimate the ROI and payback period (S1, M2).
4. Risks: communication outages, sensor failures, staff skills, data security — measures.
5. Discuss the possibilities of using state programmes and subsidies.

## Discussion questions

- How does the growth of cashless payments affect small businesses?
- Are online cash registers a burden or an opportunity for an entrepreneur?
- How does Internet quality in rural areas affect economic development (the "digital divide")?
- In which sector do you think network technologies give the greatest economic effect, and why?

:::warning
**Common mistakes**

- **Not linking the technology to a sector problem.** Not "we will introduce IoT" but "we will cut water use by 20%".
- **Ignoring coverage.** There may be no mobile Internet in the field — an alternative channel is needed.
- **Using outdated statistics.** Digital indicators change every year.
- **Forgetting security.** Connections to payment and industrial systems must be secure (M14).
:::

:::tip
Choose one company or organization in your region and list the network technologies it uses (a payment terminal, an online cash register, a website, ordering via a messenger); propose the one it is missing.
:::

## Summary

- Network technologies create value in banking, tax, trade, logistics, industry, agriculture, energy, healthcare and education.
- Payment networks and online cash registers make the economy transparent and fast.
- IoT and sensors help save resources (water, energy, fuel).
- Every solution is assessed together with the sector problem, costs and benefits, connectivity and security.
